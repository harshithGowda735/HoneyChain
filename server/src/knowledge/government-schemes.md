# HoneyChain Knowledge Base: Government Beekeeping Schemes & Subsidies

## 1. National Beekeeping & Honey Mission (NBHM)
- **Ministry**: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India (Sweet Revolution).
- **Core Objectives**: Promoting scientific beekeeping, setting up custom hiring centers, disease diagnostic labs, and honey testing labs.
- **Components**:
  - Mini Mission I: Production & productivity enhancement (subsidies on bee boxes, bee colonies, and honey extractors up to 40% to 50% for individual farmers and higher for SC/ST/Women/FPOs).
  - Mini Mission II: Post-harvest management, collection centers, cold storage, and traceability systems.
  - Mini Mission III: Research, apicultural education, and skill development.

## 2. KVIC Honey Mission
- **Agency**: Khadi and Village Industries Commission (KVIC), Ministry of MSME.
- Distribution of modern bee boxes with live bee colonies (*Apis cerana indica*) to traditional pot-hive beekeepers and rural youth along with training toolkits.

## 3. State Apiculture Initiatives (e.g. Karnataka Horticulture)
- Schemes supported by Department of Horticulture (Government of Karnataka) provide 50% capital subsidy on bee colonies, Langstroth boxes, extractors, and smoker kits.
- Verification and applications can be processed through local Senior Assistant Directors of Horticulture (SADH) or Raitha Samparka Kendras (RSK).
