# HoneyChain Knowledge Base: Honey Harvesting

## 1. When to Harvest Honey
- Harvest only fully capped honey frames (at least 75% to 80% capped with wax). Uncapped nectar has high moisture (> 20%) and will ferment into mead or spoil rapidly.
- Standard honey moisture should ideally be below 19% (FSSAI apicultural standard is ≤ 20%).
- Harvest during dry, warm mid-day hours when foragers are out in the field.

## 2. Extraction Best Practices
- Never use direct heat or boiling water on raw honey. Excessive heat (> 40°C) destroys beneficial bee enzymes (diastase, invertase) and increases HMF (Hydroxymethylfurfural) levels.
- Use a stainless steel hand or motorized centrifugal extractor.
- Filter through food-grade double mesh (coarse 18 mesh followed by fine 100 mesh) to remove wax particles while preserving microscopic pollen grains.

## 3. Recording a Harvest in HoneyChain
- Open **My Apiary** or tap **Record Harvest** on the Home screen.
- Select the specific source hive(s) from which the honey was extracted.
- Enter net extracted weight in kilograms (kg), date of extraction, honey floral source (e.g., Multifloral, Acacia, Jamun, Mustard, Wild Forest), and optional moisture percentage.
- Tapping **Save Harvest** automatically generates a traceable **Honey Batch** linked to those exact colonies.
