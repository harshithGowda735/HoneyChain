# HoneyChain Knowledge Base: App Navigation & Feature Usage

## 1. HoneyChain App Structure
- **Home**: Overview of apiary statistics (total hives, active colonies, total harvested honey, latest telemetry weight trends, and quick action shortcuts).
- **My Apiary**: Unified workspace for registered hives, recording honey harvests, tracking historical scale measurements, and managing honey traceability batches.
- **Schemes**: Official government apiculture subsidies, mission grants, documentation checklists, and application guidelines.
- **Profile**: Beekeeper profile settings, apiary name, jurisdiction/district details, multilingual preferences (Kannada, Hindi, English), and sign out.

## 2. Talk to HoneyChain Voice Support
- Tap the floating green microphone icon at the bottom right corner of the screen.
- Select your preferred language: **Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ)**, **Hindi (हिन्दी)**, or **English**.
- **Push-to-Talk**: Press and hold the microphone button to speak your question in natural language.
- Release the button to send. The assistant transcribes your audio, checks your actual registered hive telemetry, retrieves scientific knowledge, and speaks the answer back.
- **Barge-In**: If the assistant is speaking and you need to ask another question, simply press the mic button again; audio playback stops immediately.
- If you need human assistance, tap **Request Human Callback** to create an official support request for a district apiculture agent.
