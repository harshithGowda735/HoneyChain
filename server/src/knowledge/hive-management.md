# HoneyChain Knowledge Base: Hive Management

## 1. Registering a New Colony
- In HoneyChain, each hive represents a physical apicultural colony (Langstroth, Newton, or traditional box).
- To register a hive: Navigate to the **My Apiary** tab, tap **Add Hive**, enter the hive name (e.g., "North Orchard Box 1"), installation location, frame count (standard 8 or 10 frames), and initial health status.
- Once registered, a unique Hive ID is generated (e.g., `HIVE-XXXX`).

## 2. Colony Inspections and Routine Checks
- Weekly brood inspections should check for queen laying patterns, drone cells, stored pollen, and honey reserves.
- Look for uniform brood patterns. Spotty brood can indicate queen failure or nutritional stress.
- Frame counts: Ensure space is added (super boxes) before 80% of current frames are capped to prevent swarming.

## 3. Apiary Placement Guidelines
- Hives should face East or South-East to catch morning sunlight, encouraging early foraging.
- Provide windbreaks against strong seasonal drafts and ensure hives are elevated at least 30 cm off the ground to prevent dampness and predator (ant/termite) ingress.
- Ensure accessible fresh water within 500 meters of the apiary.

## 4. Seasonal Adjustments
- **Monsoon / Rainy Season**: Elevate hive entrances, ensure hive roofs are waterproof, and provide sugar syrup or pollen patties if natural foraging drops significantly.
- **Summer / Heatwaves**: Provide shade covers or canvas damp cloths over supers. Adequate ventilation is required to prevent comb melting.
- **Winter**: Reduce entrance size to retain hive heat and prevent robbing.
